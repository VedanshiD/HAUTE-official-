WELCOME THE SUN

APOLLO, the Roman god of the sun, was hopelessly lovestruck. Yet despite his handsomeness and charm, Daphne, a naiad nymph, always fled his blinding light. Desperate to win her, Apollo did the only logical thing--he wore Soleil--a scent he spun from pure sunlight, vanilla cream, and ambrosia nectar. As he entered the forest, the fragrance seemed to embrace him. Captivated by the beautiful scent in the breeze, Daphne didn't run. Instead of transforming into a laurel tree, she stepped forward, and finally, she welcomed the sun.

SUN-KISSED

SOLEIL is a solar fragnance built around a velvety, sun-drenched vanilla cream. The scent drifts between a splash of golden citrus, bright as a summer morning, and a core of warm sun nectar with a subtle note of toasted nut. An aroma sure to linger behind you, it melts into a rich base of sweet vanilla and soft amber. One spray will feel like the sun's warmth itself, and you'll smell like a sandy beach vacation without even stepping a foot into the sand.

KEY INGREDIENTS

Benzyl salicyate is a naturally derived organic compound which features a subtle, sweet balsamic scent. We also added ylang-ylang oils for additional tropical, rich, and warm undertones of custard, banana, and honey. The essential element, however was the natural extract coming from the citrus of bergamot lemon. This supplied the bright, zesty citrus punch you'll find in any lemon, with the slightly spicy undertone coming from the peels that you'll smell in the perfume. In order to make the scent last, we added ambergris, a substrance produced in the digestive system of sperm whales that is known for being a rare, effective fixative that helps perfume scents last longer.
