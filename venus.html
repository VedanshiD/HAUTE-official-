FLYTRAP

The Venus Flytrap, the muse of this piece, attracts its prey using sweet nectar, bright colors, and food-like scents. It mimics the aromas of flowers and ripening fruit. The Venus Flytrap is named after Venus, the Roman goddess of beauty and love, and this scent was subsequently named so too.

THE SWEETEST BLOOM

Our primary goal of this scent was to be able to seamlessly combine the sweet, rich aroma you're hit with when stepping into a bakery, with the intoxicating floral scent you'll find in lush flower meadows. Venus is the perfect mix this, consisting of the main floral aroma, and a subtle sweet frosting scent. The scent opens with a bright burst of ripening fruit, tempting as fresh-picked berries on a spring afternoon built around a lush, nectar-sweet floral heart. The scent settles into a whipped, honeysuckle, lingering with a fruity jam-like sweetness of a pastry filling. As the dewy, honeyed scent floats in the air, you're sure to turn heads with this scent trailing behind your footsteps.

KEY INGREDIENTS

Honeysuckle absolute froms the lush, nectar-sweet floral heart of the fragrance, capturing the same allyring aroma the Venus Flytrap used to draw in its prey. We layered Fiori di Sicilia extract, a delicate floral-vanilla note traditionally used in Italian pastry baking, to bridge the gap between blooming flower and sweet frosting. A ripe berry jam accord adds a fruity, simmered sweetness, reminiscent of pastry filling still warm the oven, while a soft whipped frosting note lends a sugared, creamy finish that lingers like the last but of dessert. Finally, we included trace organic compounds drawn from the Venus Flytrap itself, subtle understated, a quiet nod to the plant's own natural, nectar-like scent. In order to make the scent last, we added ambergris, a substance produced in the digestive system of sperm whales that is known for being a rare, effective fixative that helps perfume scents last longer.
